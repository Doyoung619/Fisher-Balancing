# Continuous-ball OFAT experiment: scientific and numerical audit

This is a new experiment implemented in this isolated project. Its action domain is the entire closed Euclidean ball. No finite candidate pool, six-axis design restriction, archived temperature constant, old warm start, coordinatewise logistic solver, or cached result from another experiment is imported. All seven active policies use the same constrained ridge estimator and response protocol.

The explicit user amendment in `sources/USER_AMENDMENT_20261005.md` removes
E-relax from the production queue. Its implementation and audit details below
are retained as historical evidence only; all seven OFAT rows and all other
settings are unchanged.

## Fixed experimental design

Exactly seven conditions occur in each of Gaussian and Bernoulli, with center `(d,L,m,kappa)=(10,1,10,1.5)`, dimensions 5/20, radii 0.5/2, and batches 5/20 changing one coordinate each. The same center artifacts feed every OFAT panel. The production horizon is 20,000 responses for every trajectory, with 512 repetitions at center and 256 elsewhere. Thus there are 98 family/condition/method combinations and 28,672 logical trajectories. Preview uses the first 32 repetitions through N=2,000 and continues their exact states. No extension based on the observed ranking is automatic.

One stored generic unit direction per dimension is generated from an independent environment seed. Truth is `theta_star=(kappa/L)*u_d`, with norms 1.5, 3, or 0.75, all strictly below the parameter radius 4. Both families share directions, and same-d radius/batch comparisons use the same direction. Acquisition receives neither the true direction nor the theoretical reference. Radius changes hold kappa fixed and rho=1; they do not assert exact finite-N scale invariance.

Both families start with no observations, theta_hat=0 and Vhat=Vstar=I. The Gaussian conditional variance is 1. The Bernoulli response is canonical logistic. There is no intercept or hidden feature rescaling. All m actions are selected using frozen observed data before m fresh responses are generated, followed by one constrained refit.

## Estimator and information audit

The optimized objective is the SUM of negative log likelihood plus `rho*||theta||²/2`, subject to `||theta||<=4`; rho is 1 throughout. Gaussian fits retain exact C=sum(xxᵀ), b=sum(xy). The unconstrained solve is accepted only when feasible; otherwise an eigenbasis secular solve finds eta>=0 satisfying `||(C+(rho+eta)I)^-1 b||=4`. Radial clipping of an anisotropic unconstrained fit is not used.

Bernoulli fits retain the complete actual continuous X/y history and evaluate its exact logaddexp objective, gradient, and Hessian at each Newton iterate. Each quadratic Newton model is minimized on the actual parameter ball, with a likelihood Armijo line search. A constrained SLSQP fallback is followed by explicit stationarity checks. Reusing the previous estimate is a numerical warm start, not extra warm-start observations. No nearby queries are merged and no stale weighted sufficient statistic replaces logistic history.

For g=gradient and a nonnegative radial multiplier eta, the recorded KKT criterion is the maximum of `||g+eta*theta||/max(1,N)`, positive feasibility violation, and `eta*abs(||theta||-4)/max(1,N)`. eta is estimated from the inward radial gradient when the solution is within 1e-7 of the boundary. Target residual is 1e-8. A small step does not imply success; failed final checks fail the job and retain failure evidence instead of silently contributing an invalid fit. Boundary counts, failures, maximum residuals, iteration counts, and fallback flags are logged.

The final Hessian is `Vhat=rho I+Xᵀ diag(w(X theta_hat_current)) X`. Thus all old predictors and weights are recomputed under the current fit at every batch boundary. Vstar adds true-parameter increments only in the evaluator. They coincide for Gaussian within floating point error. H for UCB is the observed unweighted Gram plus ridge.

The chosen coefficient never changes with preview, resumption or continuation. Every FB temperature is saved. ## Continuous numerical approximations

The logistic quadratic objective uses `x=z u+U y`, where u is the *current fitted* direction, never the truth. For each z, a symmetric eigensystem and secular equation solve the conditional quadratic maximization globally, including hard cases. A normalized scalar search over z/L in [0,1], using evenness, brackets/refines detected peaks. It includes positive-to-negative envelope-derivative brackets and one-sided endpoint derivatives, so narrow maxima near the endpoints are not missed solely by a value grid. A prelaunch stricter audit exposed such a value-only miss; before/after evidence is retained in the oracle audit artifacts. The default scalar grid has 17 points and is an initialization/refinement device, not a finite query domain. Independent direct-coordinate multistart audits use denser scalar grids and stricter optimization. A finite grid, even with local refinement, is not a mathematical full-domain upper certificate.

A-opt and E-greedy use normalized action coordinates x=L*v with a genuine ball constraint, analytic derivatives of their appropriate smooth expressions, eigenvector/current-solution starts and dedicated random starts. Their production defaults and any profile-selected numerical settings are recorded in the immutable configuration. Feasibility tolerance is `1e-9*max(1,L)`. Audited score discrepancies normalize the positive audited-minus-original score by `max(1e-14,abs(audited_score))`, targeting 1e-6; failed audits refine the selected point and retain pre-refinement loss. The label-independent audit schedule includes the initial batch and every 200th batch. Exact Gaussian solutions and analytic UCB are separately identified. The final prelaunch strict stress audit covers 25 batches: maximum PSD-objective normalized discrepancy is about 2.5e-11 and maximum E-primary discrepancy about 1.9e-9; these are empirical checks, not global certificates. E-greedy near-multiplicity diagnostics report the actual dense primary value and permit at most the recorded small multiplicity gap, rather than reporting a zero-loss exact tie for unequal eigenvalues.

The following three paragraphs document the excluded historical E-relax implementation.

E-relax reuses its symbolic SDP and checkpointed support/weights, but sets Clarabel solver warm starts to false so hidden process-local solver state cannot affect resume or worker-order reproducibility. A regression with fresh one-worker/two-worker pools and interrupted continuation compares actual E-relax histories, estimates and RNG states, to 1e-8 numerical tolerance.

The E-relax restricted primal bound is recomputed from nonnegative, renormalized probabilities and actual current support matrices. A dual matrix is projected to PSD and normalized to trace one. The local separation value yields an *empirical* generation residual. The independent bound `w<=1/4` and `xᵀP x<=L² lambda_max(P)` gives a valid, possibly loose full-ball upper bound; its certified residual is recorded separately. The initial empirical relative generation tolerance is 1e-4 with scale `max(1,abs(restricted_primal))`; finite SDP solver tolerance and iteration/cap limits are explicit. Reaching empirical tolerance does not certify the 1e-4 continuous optimum. Capped or nonconverged solves remain labeled and are never replaced by another method or skipped between batches.

The final generation limit is 256 with support caps 44/64/211 for dimensions 5/10/20. Sparse retained supports, the current fitted-parameter latitude and current information eigenvectors initialize the solve. Dynamic SDP buckets avoid unnecessary padding. CLARABEL uses one thread, numerical warm starts disabled, and the measured faer/qdldl routing in the original local oracle audit (not distributed). At full support cardinality, affine nullspace exchanges preserve total probability and the current Fisher matrix while admitting another continuous separator; residuals are checked and logged. This corrected the high cap rate observed in the initial 24-generation prototype. All 56 final early/late profile solves met the empirical tolerance; this is numerical evidence, not a full-domain certificate.

The manuscript assumes exact acquisition maxima. Therefore Bernoulli results from the practical scalar/multistart oracles are numerical experiments against its targets, not a proof that this approximation inherits all theorem conclusions. This limitation is distinct from the independently certified theoretical reference, whose relative bound gap is below 1e-7.

## Randomness, exact Gaussian sharing, and reproducibility

Master seed is 20261005. Hashed indexed streams separate environment construction, response innovations, solver starts, tie handling, rounding, TS, and random sampling. Response innovations depend only on family/d/replicate/global observation index: methods consume the same standard normals or uniforms but apply them at their own queries. Policy randomness cannot advance responses. Independent replicate IDs have independent streams. The environment direction is fixed across response repetitions.

With constant Gaussian curvature, FB, A-opt, D-opt and E-greedy choose exactly the same deterministic radius-L minimum-eigenspace actions under the common tie convention. The canonical tie projects the first usable coordinate axis into the whole minimum eigenspace. These paths are response-independent. Shared noise plus the identical estimator yields exactly identical histories, fits, information and risks, so one Ours physical job supplies all four logical method artifacts through explicit alias records/hardlinks. This saves 6,144 physical jobs: 28,672 logical trajectories correspond to 22,528 physical trajectories. Aliases are not claimed as independent runs. All four plotted labels and metadata remain available. The shared NPZ may include Ours temperature trace; A/D/E do not use temperature in their acquisition.

`src/design_cache.py` additionally reuses the exact cyclic Gaussian design across repetitions and batch sizes. Its key includes dimension, radius, the canonical tie convention, and the absence of policy randomness. Independent tests compare it with all four full-ball objectives in every OFAT row. The Gaussian coordinate actions follow from analytic optimization over the entire ball, not an imposed finite candidate set. Separate trajectories still refit at every requested batch boundary and retain their full responses.

Random, TS and UCB are not cloned across independent repetitions. No cross-batch-condition trajectory sharing is currently applied; same-center figures reuse data without extra repetitions. Worker scheduling does not seed any policy; safe-boundary checkpoints save X/y, sufficient statistics, theta, information, method supports, every RNG state, completed metric records, tau history, and source/config/code compatibility. Same-run interrupted continuation is checked against uninterrupted trajectories, including E-relax with fresh solver processes and different worker counts. Ordinary process interruption may lose work after the last durable batch checkpoint but cannot switch seeds or silently create a new trajectory.

## References, metrics, and output interpretation

Before performance evaluation, a standalone evaluator computes Gaussian gamma=L²/d. For every requested Bernoulli condition it proves `gamma=L²*w(kappa/sqrt(d))/d` and M*=gamma I, using rotational symmetrization, boundary completion, a strictly positive-definite tight dual, and a monotone derivative certificate. A distinct adaptive support LP verifies a feasible lower bound to relative 1e-7. Outward interval evaluation of the analytic formula gives near-machine-precision lower/upper values. Matrix uniqueness is analytically established for these particular conditions, rather than inferred from isotropy. See the full derivation and caveats in [audit_theory.md](audit_theory.md).

Uncentered Q_hat is the mean of all error outer products; its trace equals mean squared error. Every information eigenvalue is taken before averaging across repetitions; the directional eigenvalue is taken after averaging error outer products. Required panels retain their prescribed 2x2 order. N=0 is saved separately and omitted from log axes. Raw/normalized/ridge-removed information, raw/scaled risks, Q_hat, mean error, information spectra, and tracking norms are saved. The true finite-N ceiling is gamma+rho/N; no identical plug-in ceiling is claimed.

MC SEM is used only for information means and mean squared Euclidean error. Directional precision is evaluated using stored error vectors, prefix/disjoint-block stability, and full-statistic resampling. Resampling quantiles are descriptive, not guaranteed 95% confidence intervals for a repeated population eigenvalue. No centering, dimension division, clipping, smoothing toward targets, symmetrization, shrinkage or debiasing is applied. Paired method differences preserve repetition IDs. Endpoint tables report the actual repetition count and distinguish finite-N discrepancy, MC uncertainty, reference error and numerical oracle qualifications.

Plots cover both families, all seven active methods, all seven conditions, reused-center dimension/radius/batch comparisons, Ours normalized summaries, tracking, runtime and numerical diagnostics. Every figure is PDF plus PNG, with underlying CSV/NPZ and provenance manifest. Incomplete previews use completed consistent checkpoint groups and explicitly label missing comparisons. Failures are retained in summaries; no unfavorable trajectory is silently removed. The supervisor produces preview and final plots automatically and final figures are not claimed complete until the requested queue is complete.
